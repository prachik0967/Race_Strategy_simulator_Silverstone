# Objective

The objective of this project is to develop a  race strategy simulator using python for the Silverstone Grand Prix Circuit (British GP).

I aim to make a simulator that will model the effect of tyre compound, tyre degradation, fuel load as well as include the pit stop loss in the total race time.

Hopefully, I will also be able to determine which combination of tyre compounds and pit stop timings produce the lowest simulated race time.


# Circuit parameters

- Circuit: Silverstone Grand Prix Circuit
- Circuit length: 5.891 km
- Race laps: 52
- Race distance: 306.198 km

# Initial Model requirements and scope

- Soft, Medium and Hard tyre compounds
- Tyre degradation
- Fuel mass reduction as the race progresses
- Fuel related effects on lap time
- Pit stop time loss
- One-stop strategies
- Two-stop strategies

- it will not include random race events such as weather, Safety Cars, traffic or driver errors.

# Inputs

- Race lap
- Tyre compound
- Tyre age
- Fuel mass
- Fuel consumption
- Pit-stop lap
- Pit-stop time loss

# Outputs

- Individual lap times
- Stint times
- Total race time
- Pit-stop strategy
- Tyre compound sequence
- Optimal simulated race strategy
