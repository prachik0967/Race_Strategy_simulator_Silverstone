# Overview

This section presents the main outputs from the deterministic optimisation and Monte Carlo simulations.

The results are used to compare tyre strategies, pit-stop timing and the effect of uncertainty on predicted race performance.

The key outputs include:

- Tyre degradation behaviour
- Fuel mass reduction
- Optimal one-stop strategy
- Optimal two-stop strategy
- One-stop versus two-stop comparison
- Pit-window sensitivity
- Monte Carlo race-time distribution
- Probability of one strategy outperforming another


# Deterministic Results

The deterministic optimisation evaluates multiple tyre compound combinations and pit-stop timings.

The strategy with the minimum calculated race time is identified as the deterministic optimum.

Both one-stop and two-stop strategies are compared.



# Monte Carlo Results

The Monte Carlo model repeats race simulations with random lap-time variation.

This produces a distribution of possible race times rather than a single predicted value.

The Monte Carlo analysis is used to estimate:

- Mean race time
- Standard deviation
- Range of simulated race times
- Probability of one strategy outperforming another



# Interpretation

The results should not be treated as exact predictions of real Formula 1 performance.

Instead, they demonstrate how tyre degradation, fuel load and pit-stop timing interact within the simplified race model.
